About the Author

Rajul Tiwari is a distinguished Indian author, psychologist, publisher, editor, and educator based in Delhi NCR. Originally from Gorakhpur, Uttar Pradesh, she brings over 20 years of rich experience across creative writing, media publishing, education, and strategic content development.

Served as first-draft editor for Veer Gatha, an NCERT supplementary book about India's Param Vir Chakra awardees. As a research scholar in Criminal Psychology, her journey seamlessly integrates literature, human behavior, and educational mentorship.
