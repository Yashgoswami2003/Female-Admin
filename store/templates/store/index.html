About the Author

Rajul Tiwari is an Indian author, psychologist, publisher, editor, and educator based in Delhi NCR. Originally from Gorakhpur, Uttar Pradesh, she brings over 20 years of experience across writing, publishing, media, education, and content creation.

She holds an MA in English, an MA in Psychology, and a BEd degree, and is a research scholar in Criminal Psychology. Her professional journey reflects her interests in literature, human behaviour, education, and communication.
